# Carbon Calories

## Why use it?

Carbon Calories allows for smart, AI-powered carbon insights on both a personal and a commercial scale, tracked numerically and with a smart "traffic light" system that helps users new to carbon footprint tracking. 
Carbon Calories allows you to log your meals similar to existing calorie trackers like MyFitnessPal, but instead of nutrition data, it tracks carbon footprint. 
Log your meals, get ideas to improve your footprint from our AI chef, and see your data over time in our analytics tab.
